The prescription

Which mesh do you need?

Five meshes, each suited to a different job. Not sure which? Tell us what tore the old one.

MeshWhat it's forFits
Standard fibreglass Everyday insect screening. The usual choice for windows and light-use doors. Flyscreens
316 marine grade stainless roll mesh Tougher than fibreglass and resists corrosion, so it suits coastal homes. Flyscreens & doors
316 marine grade security mesh Woven stainless for security doors, with a clear view out. Security doors
Limited vision aluminium (Visionguard) You can see out; it is harder for passers-by to see in. Privacy
Perforated aluminium A solid punched sheet that stands up to knocks and still lets air through. Security doors

Common questions

Do I need a new door, or just new mesh?

Usually just new mesh. If the frame is sound, we replace the mesh in it on site. If the door itself is past it, we can supply and fit a new one.

How much does it cost to replace flyscreen mesh?

It depends on the size of the screen and the mesh you choose. Send a photo and rough measurements and we'll give you a price before we visit. Quotes are free.

How long does it take?

Most jobs are finished in a single visit. Adam to confirm typical times

Can you fix the lock or hinges while you're here?

Yes. We repair locks and hinges on security doors and screen doors.
