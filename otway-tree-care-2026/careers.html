What to expect

What working with us is like

Real skills, not just labouring

Train alongside a qualified arborist and learn climbing, rigging and machine operation on real jobs.

Well‑kept gear

Chipper, tracked stump grinder, mulcher and trucks that are properly maintained, so you can do the job right.

Local work

Jobs across Colac, Apollo Bay and the Otways — coast, farms and forest, often in the same week.

A small, family‑run team

Straight talk, and work that gets noticed. You’ll always know where you stand.

Room to grow

Pick up your climbing and machine tickets and build a career in the trade.

Safety done properly

A Safe Work Method Statement for every job, proper PPE and maintained equipment.
