Good to know

Property maintenance: common questions

Ask us something else

How often would you visit?

Whatever suits the property. Some sites need monthly visits, others a seasonal clean-up before summer and after winter storms. We’ll suggest a schedule after walking the site.

Can you look after my property while I’m away?

Yes — that’s what scheduled visits are for. We agree what gets checked and done on each visit, and let you know if we find anything that needs attention.

Do you do one-off clean-ups?

Yes. Plenty of properties start with a big clean-up and then move to lighter regular visits.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $20 million public liability insurance for arboricultural work, and WorkCover for our staff. Every job is planned with a Safe Work Method Statement (SWMS) before work starts.
