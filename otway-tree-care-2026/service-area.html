Colac · Elliminyt · Alvie · Beeac · Birregurra

Colac & district

Colac is home base, so jobs around town, the lakes and the surrounding farms are close by. Expect everything from backyard removals and pruning to windbreaks, fence lines and stumps.

Local rule to know: Birregurra has its own Tree Protection Area, where a permit is needed to remove most trees — exotic as well as native. And across Colac’s residential areas, the state’s canopy-tree rules can apply to larger trees near the front or rear boundary.

Council: Colac Otway Shire

Lorne · Wye River · Skenes Creek · Apollo Bay · Marengo

Great Ocean Road

Coastal gums and cypress take a hammering from salt wind, and many blocks are steep and tight. Plenty of homes are holiday houses and the owners aren’t always there — tell us, and we’ll arrange access and keep you in the loop by phone.

Local rules to know: Skenes Creek, Kennett River, Wye River and Separation Creek sit in a landscape overlay where removing or lopping a tree usually needs a permit. Apollo Bay has its own landscape overlays for native vegetation, and Lorne is in Surf Coast Shire, with different rules again.

Council: Colac Otway Shire · Surf Coast Shire (Lorne)

Forrest · Barwon Downs · Gellibrand · Beech Forest · Lavers Hill

Otway Ranges

Up in the ranges it’s tall timber, tree ferns, wet gullies and soft ground. Access and weather decide a lot here, and hazard trees near houses, sheds and tracks need careful reading before anyone cuts.

Local rule to know: much of the ranges is covered by a landslip (erosion management) overlay, where removing vegetation generally needs a permit, with limited exemptions.

Council: Colac Otway Shire
