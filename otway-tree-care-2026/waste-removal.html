Green waste removal · Colac & the Otways

Branch piles and green waste, chipped and cleared

After a storm, a clearing job or a big garden cut-back, we chip the branches on site and either leave the mulch or take it away. Logs can be cut to firewood length, stacked or carted off.

Who calls us for green waste

Homeowners

After DIY pruning, a garden overhaul or a storm that’s left branches everywhere.

Landholders

How it works

  • Chipped on siteBranches go through the chipper, which turns a big pile into a much smaller load of mulch.
  • Heavy material moved by machineLogs and big limbs are handled with the tracked loader rather than by hand.
  • Keep it or cart itKeep the mulch for garden beds or erosion control, or have it taken away.
  • Logs your wayCut to firewood length and stacked, left as habitat logs, or removed.

Good to know

Green waste removal: common questions

Ask us something else

What can you take?

Branches, prunings, hedge clippings, logs and stumps. We don’t take household rubbish, soil, or treated or painted timber. If you’re not sure, send us a photo.

Is it cheaper to keep the mulch?

Usually, yes — leaving it on site saves the cost of carting it away, and it’s useful on garden beds and bare ground.

Can you clean up after someone else’s job?

Yes. Tell us what’s been left and we’ll quote to process and clear it.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the volume, the size of the material, access and whether it stays or goes: size, species and condition, how close it is to buildings or lines, how easy it is to get gear in, and what you want done with the wood, chips and stump. That’s why we look first. The site assessment is free and your quote is a fixed price for the agreed work — the price we quote is the price you pay.
