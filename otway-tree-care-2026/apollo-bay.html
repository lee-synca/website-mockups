Permits · Colac Otway Shire

Tree rules around Apollo Bay

Apollo Bay, Skenes Creek and Marengo are in Colac Otway Shire. Several local rules decide whether a tree can go without a permit.

To see which overlays apply to your block, search your address on VicPlan. Then check with Colac Otway Shire on 03 5232 9400.

  • Apollo Bay landscape areas: a permit is needed for native vegetation. Dead vegetation and pruning up to 20% of a plant are exempt.
  • Skenes Creek: a landscape overlay requires a permit to remove or lop a tree. Small trunks (under 0.5 m around at 1 m high), dead or dying trees, and pruning for regrowth or shaping are exempt.
  • Landslip areas: much of the Great Ocean Road coast is in an erosion management overlay, where removing any vegetation generally needs a permit.
  • Town blocks: since September 2025, larger trees near the front or rear boundary of a residential or township lot can need a permit. The rules changed again on 24 September 2026.

When we look at a job, we’ll tell you if we think a permit is likely and point you to the council. We don’t lodge applications or write formal arborist reports, and approval is always the council’s decision. Our permit guide has the details.

A plain-English summary, not legal or planning advice. Rules change, so always confirm with council before work starts.
