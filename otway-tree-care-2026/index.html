Good to know

Questions people ask before they call

Ask us something else

Are quotes and site assessments free?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree and the site with you, and then give you a fixed-price quote for the work you want. There’s no charge for the visit and no obligation to go ahead.

What does a fixed-price quote cover?

Everything written in it: the work, the method, the clean-up, and what happens to the timber, chips and stump. The price we quote is the price you pay. If you ask for extra work on the day, we’ll price it with you before doing it.

Do I need a council permit to remove or prune a tree?

Often, yes. Most native vegetation in the Otways is protected, many properties sit under extra overlays (landscape, vegetation or landslip), and since September 2025 some large trees on town blocks need a permit too. Whether your tree needs one depends on the tree and exactly where your property is.

Check with council before work is booked — Colac Otway Shire (03 5232 9400) for most of the region, or Surf Coast Shire (03 5261 0600) for Lorne. If we think a permit is likely when we look at the job, we’ll tell you. Our plain-English permit guide explains the main rules.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $20 million public liability insurance for arboricultural work, and WorkCover for our staff. Every job is planned with a Safe Work Method Statement (SWMS) before work starts.

What should I do if a tree comes down?

Get everyone clear, then call 0448 762 058 — the line is answered 24/7. If power lines are involved, stay at least 10 metres away and call Powercor on 13 24 12. If anyone is in danger, call 000. More on storm and emergency work.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Colac and work across the Otway Ranges and the Great Ocean Road — including Colac, Elliminyt, Birregurra, Beeac, Alvie, Lorne, Wye River, Skenes Creek, Apollo Bay, Marengo, Forrest, Barwon Downs, Gellibrand, Beech Forest and Lavers Hill, plus Winchelsea and Camperdown. If you’re just outside these areas, ask. See the service area.

How quickly will you get back to me?

We aim to reply to every non-emergency enquiry within 24 hours. For anything dangerous — a tree down, split or hanging over the house — call 0448 762 058 any time. The line is answered 24/7.
