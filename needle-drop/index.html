How it works

From your mix to their ears in three moves

  1. 1

    Upload your mix

    Drop in the MP3 and the tracklist. Needle Drop finds where each song starts, and you fine-tune it on the waveform.

  2. 2

    Share one link

    Fans add your app to their home screen from a link or a QR code at the gig. No app store, no approval wait.

  3. 3

    Get paid

    Fans hear a free preview, then buy the mix or stream everything monthly. The money goes straight to you.

Features

Everything you need to sell your mixes

No more sending MP3s by email or Google Drive links and hoping people come back. Your app keeps them listening, and keeps them buying.

Your app

It feels like the apps they already use

Your newest drop up top, shelves by vibe, a library, search and a player, on phones and computers. Nothing new for your fans to learn, and it's all you.

  • Your name, your covers and your colours.
  • Lock-screen controls, and downloads that play with no signal.

Tracklists

Tap a song, drop the needle

Your mix stays one continuous file with no gaps, but every song is one tap away. The player always shows what's playing now, here and on the lock screen.

  • On computers each song's start time is shown too, so fans can jump straight to their favourite.
  • The record spins while it plays.
  • Works on phones and computers, from the same link.

Your Control Centre

Mark every song on the waveform

Upload a mix and Needle Drop finds where the songs change. Open the full-screen editor to check them: Space plays, Ctrl and scroll zooms, and you drag a marker to the exact beat.

  • Paste a tracklist, or read it off the back cover.
  • Set the price, the preview length and the release date for each mix.

Selling

It sells while you sleep

Fans hear the first ten minutes free, then buy the mix to keep, or stream every mix on a monthly plan. Automatic offers do the rest, and your dashboard shows what's coming in.

  • Offers when someone plays a mix on repeat, plays two in a row, or goes quiet.
  • Bundles, sales and promo codes, with what each one earned.

Questions

Good to know

Can I use it now?

Needle Drop is being tested with the first DJs we built it for. We're opening it up a few at a time, so join the early-access list and we'll be in touch when there's a spot.

What does it cost?

We haven't set pricing yet. Joining the early-access list is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

Do my fans need to download anything from an app store?

No. You share a link or a QR code, and they add your app to their home screen in a few taps. It opens full screen with your icon, like any other app, and there's a step-by-step guide built in.

Can I sell mixes that use other people's songs?

Only music you have the rights to sell or stream. That's your call and your responsibility, and Needle Drop doesn't sort out licensing for you. Your Control Centre keeps a private record of what's been cleared for each mix, so you can keep track.

Is the demo the real thing?

It's the same app with a made-up DJ. There's no audio in the demo and mixes play fast, so you can see everything in a minute. Nothing is charged or sent. There's a demo of the Control Centre too: open it here.
