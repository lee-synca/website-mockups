Kia ora, and thanks for your time. This is a short introduction to AVEA: who we are, what we do for artists, how it works and what we will and won't promise. Ask questions any time.

Who we are

A Pacific-owned music company.

AVEA is a Pacific-owned music distribution and artist services company. We help artists get their music out, understand their rights and build careers without losing what makes them who they are.

The deal

The deal, in plain words.

Before you send us anything, here is how working with AVEA goes.

  • There's no fee to apply.Our standard services use a revenue-share model, and we'll explain the relevant fees and splits before you agree to anything.
  • You keep ownership of your mastersunder our standard distribution arrangement. We'll explain the rights we need to work with your music before you sign.
  • Nothing is signed until you understand it.We'll talk you through the terms and splits first. Bring your family, your manager or your lawyer.
  • We won't promise streams, playlists or fame.Nobody honestly can. What we offer is proper work, straight answers and clear reporting.
