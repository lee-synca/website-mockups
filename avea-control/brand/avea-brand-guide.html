Who we are

Our heartbeat

We champion the next wave of Pacific talent.

Pacific artists have been reaching audiences around the world for years. We want the support behind the music to catch up. AVEA brings together the tools, people and know-how to help artists make their next move while staying connected to where they come from.

Making music is one part of the job. Getting it released, heard and paid for takes a lot of work too. That's where we come in.

04

Tone of voice

Say it straight. Promise only what we control.

Artists have heard big promises before. AVEA earns trust by being exact. These four rules come straight from the approved website copy, and the same rules power the wording check in the AVEA Control Centre.

Describe what we do

Do"We help deliver your releases to major streaming and download platforms."

Don't"Your music on every platform worldwide."

Qualify ownership honestly

Do"Under our standard distribution arrangement, you keep ownership of your masters."

Don't"You keep everything, always."

Never promise results

Do"We can't promise streams, playlists or particular results. We'll be clear about what we can do."

Don't"We'll get you on playlists and make it go viral."

Be clear about cost

Do"There's no fee to apply. We'll explain the fees and splits before you agree to anything."

Don't"It's completely free."

Call to action

"Apply to Join AVEA". Not "Sign up" or "Get started".

Spelling

New Zealand English: optimise, catalogue, colour. "Aotearoa New Zealand" in full on first mention.

Punctuation

Spaced en dashes ( – ), not hyphens. Sentence case for body text; capitals for headings and labels.

Logo

Three versions, one shape.

The letters are cut from clean, open strokes. Inside the first A sits the gold peak, a patterned chevron that carries the Pacific into the mark. Use the full lockup wherever there's room.

Don'ts

The logo only works if it looks the same every time. If it doesn't fit, change the layout, not the logo.

07

Brand colours

Deep ocean, warm gold.

Navy does most of the work, so the gold stands out every time it appears. Sand and Foam give lighter pages a warm, paper-like feel instead of plain white. Click any colour to copy its code.

Roughly how much of each

Abyss 55%Ocean 20%Sand · Foam 12%Gold 8%

Pairs that are easy to read

Contrast ratios against the international accessibility guideline (WCAG AA): 4.5 or more for normal text, 3 or more for large headings.

Foam on AbyssAll text16.5
Gold on AbyssText, labels, lines9.9
Abyss on GoldButtons9.9
Gold on FoamNever. Unreadable1.4

CMYK values are converted from screen colours. Ask the printer for a proof, especially for Gold and the navies.

Graphic system

The peak.

The gold peak inside the A is drawn out into a set of nested chevron lines. It's AVEA's signature: a mountain to climb, a roof that shelters, an arrow pointing up.

  • Gold only, on navyNever at full strength on Sand or Foam.
  • Go big and crop itLet it rise from the bottom edge, often cut off at the sides.
  • Fade it behind text10–25% opacity so words stay easy to read.
  • One per layoutIt's a signature, not a pattern. The small triangle does the repeating work.
