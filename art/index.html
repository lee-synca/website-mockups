The story

From bedroom harmonies to the world stage.

Anastasia, Rosetta and T‑R3X started out singing in their bedrooms in Wellington. Viral YouTube covers became original music with IKON Music — a sound blending R&B, island soul and Poly reggae.

They’ve performed for the Duke and Duchess of Sussex and represented Aotearoa on official Prime Ministerial delegations to Samoa and Tonga in 2026.
