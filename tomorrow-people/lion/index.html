Story

Home studio to number one

Tomorrow People are Māori and Samoan musicians from Wellington who manage themselves, produce their own records and named the band after a Ziggy Marley song.

  1. MMXVIII

    They called it BBQ reggae. So did we.

    A critic used "BBQ reggae" as an insult for the sound. The band put the phrase on the cover, drew the critic at the grill and took the record to No.1. It won at the Waiata Māori Music Awards, the Pacific Music Awards and the New Zealand Music Awards.

English

Souljah Feeling, Don't Wanna Fight It, Fever, Say My Name.

Te reo Māori

Kua To Te Ra, Rere Te Wa, Ōku Rā, Ki Raro.

Gagana Samoa

Sa'ili Le Alofa, winner of Best Pacific Language in 2018.
