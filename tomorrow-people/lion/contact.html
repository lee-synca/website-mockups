Press

The short version

Tomorrow People are a sunshine reggae band from Wellington, Aotearoa New Zealand, formed in 2010 by Avina Kelekolio, Tana Tupai, Lio Fili and Liam Va'ai.

Three of their records have debuted at No.1 on the New Zealand chart: ONE (2012, certified Gold), Bass & Bassinets (2015) and BBQ Reggae (2018). They have won Best Pacific Group four times and sing in English, te reo Māori and gagana Samoa.

The band produces its own records and is managed by Ikon Music.
