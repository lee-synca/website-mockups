Story

Home studio to number one

Tomorrow People are Māori and Samoan musicians from Wellington who write and produce their own records and named the band after a Ziggy Marley song.

  1. 2010

    Four friends and a YouTube channel

    Avina Kelekolio, Tana Tupai, Lio Fili and Liam Va'ai leave covers behind, start writing in Avina's home studio and post the demos online. The first show is a support slot in Masterton.

  2. 2012

    ONE goes straight to No.1

    The debut album tops the New Zealand chart, is certified Gold and wins Best Roots Reggae Album at the Waiata Māori Music Awards.

  3. 2013

    Best Pacific Group, the first time

    The ONE.5 EP follows the album into the top ten, and the band wins Best Pacific Group at the Pacific Music Awards. They win it again in 2018, 2019 and 2020.

  4. 2015

    Bass & Bassinets makes it two

    The second full album also debuts at No.1.

  5. MMXVIII

    They called it BBQ reggae. So did we.

    A critic used "BBQ reggae" as an insult for the sound. The band put the phrase on the cover, drew the critic at the grill and took the record to No.1. It won at the Waiata Māori Music Awards, the Pacific Music Awards and the New Zealand Music Awards.

  6. 2021

    A new song on the first Friday of every month

    A year of monthly singles became the 16-track album 21, which won Best Pacific Album.

  7. 2023

    Ki Raro, Homegrown and Samoa

    New waiata in te reo Māori, a hometown Homegrown set on the Wellington waterfront and the Motherland Festival in Samoa.
