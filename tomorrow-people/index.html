Our story

Rooted in Aotearoa

Tomorrow People draw from reggae, soul and the stories of home — countless hits, awards and plaques to their name, and one of the most beloved reggae bands to come out of Aotearoa.

The music speaks to love, unity, the land and each other. This is our kaupapa. This is our sound.
