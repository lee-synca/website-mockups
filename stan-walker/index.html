Who I am. Follow the awa from the valley it starts in to the ocean it reaches.

Te Urewera

Ruatoki

Tūhoe country. The valley, the maunga and the marae the songs keep going back to, and the place Stan calls his safe space.

Tauranga Moana

Mauao

Raised on a marae at the foot of the mountain. Church at fifteen. A voice already too big for the room.

2009 · Ahitereiria

Across the ditch

Wins Australian Idol at nineteen. Platinum records follow. So does the advice to be less Māori if he wants to last.

2021 · Hoki mai

Home again

Te Arohanui, his songs re-sung in te reo Māori. Then Māori Ki Te Ao, written in the Tūhoe dialect and filmed with three generations of his people.

2026 · Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa

Out to the ocean

Tahiti. Taputapuātea with the whānau. American Samoa. A Moana album with his own kids on the record. The river reaches the sea.
