How every job runs

No surprises, start to finish.

The same five steps on every job — a backyard prune or a public-land program.

Step 01

Site visit & assessment

We look at the tree, the ground and the access before anything else. Advice starts with what the tree needs.

Step 02

Fixed quote, in writing

One price for the whole job. What you’re quoted is what you pay — no day rates, no extras.

Step 03

Work method & safety plan

How the work will happen, documented — rigging, machinery, exclusion zones and what to expect on the day.

Step 04

The work — controlled

Planned cuts, low-impact machinery, and a crew that treats your property like their own.

Step 05

Spotless handover

Timber and green waste dealt with as agreed, the site raked and left cleaner than we found it.

If a tree needs less work than you expected, we’ll tell you that too — sometimes the right answer is a prune, not a removal.
