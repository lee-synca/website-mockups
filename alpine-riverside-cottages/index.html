The neighbours

Who you might meet

The grounds back onto bushland, so some of our regulars have feathers, spines or a bill.

  • At your window
    King parrots and rosellas

    Feeding outside most mornings.

  • In the river
    Platypus

    Sometimes seen gliding past the bank.

  • In the garden
    Echidnas

    Searching the garden beds for lunch.

  • After dark
    Possums

    Sneaking snacks from the bird feeders.

  • At dawn
    Mountain wallabies

    Out early, with the dawn chorus.

Wildlife keeps its own hours, so we can't promise a sighting.
