In the cottages

Do I need to bring linen or towels?

No. Linen and towels are supplied in every cottage.

Can I cook?

Yes. The cottages and studios have a full kitchen with an oven, stove top, microwave and full-size fridge. The two Mt Buffalo Motel Rooms have a kitchenette with a microwave, kettle, toaster and bar fridge. There are also free barbecues in the garden.

How many people does each cottage sleep?

Riverfront Studios and Mt Buffalo Motel Rooms sleep two. Garden View Cottages sleep three. The Family Riverview and Family Lakeview Cottages sleep six, in two bedrooms. Compare the cottages.

Is there heating and cooling?

Yes, every cottage has both, so it's comfortable in January and in July.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, and it's free.

Can I smoke?

No. The whole property is non-smoking.

Dogs

Can I bring my dog?

Small dogs are welcome in two of our ten cottages. Conditions and a small charge apply, and because there are only two, ring us before you book. The dogs welcome page has the rules.

Around the property

Can we swim in the river?

Yes. In summer it's deep enough to swim or drift along in a tube, and shallow enough at the edge for small children to paddle with a parent beside them. It's a natural river with no lifeguard, so please watch children at all times.

What is there for children?

Swings, a playground and plenty of lawn to run on, plus the river in summer.

Is there a laundry?

Yes, there are shared laundry facilities. The family cottages also have their own washing machine.

Where can I keep bikes or dry ski gear?

There's bicycle storage and a ski drying room on site.

The area

How far is Bright?

8 km, about ten minutes by car, or a ride along the Rail Trail.

Can I walk to anything?

Yes. Feathertop Winery and Ringer Reef Winery are a short stroll away, and the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail is across the road.

How far is the snow?

Mount Buffalo is about 40 minutes away for snow play and tobogganing in winter.

Still not sure about something?

Ask us. It's quicker than guessing.
