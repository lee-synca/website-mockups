A king bed for a night or two.

Rooms 7 and 8 are simpler than our cottages: one comfortable room with a king bed, a kitchenette for breakfast and a bathroom with a shower over a small bath.

They suit a short stay on the way through, or a couple who'll spend the days on the Rail Trail or up the mountain.

Outside your door

The river, the trail and the mountain

  • The river. Swim, paddle or drift in a tube in summer.
  • The Rail Trail. Across the road. Bike storage is on site.
  • The mountain. Mount Buffalo is about 40 minutes away. There's a ski drying room on site.
  • The wineries. Feathertop and Ringer Reef are a short stroll away.
