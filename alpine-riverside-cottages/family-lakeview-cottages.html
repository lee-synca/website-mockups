Cottages 3 & 4 · up to six · looking onto the lily pond

Family Lakeview Cottages

Two bedrooms and a pond full of fish.

Cottages 3 and 4 look onto the lily pond in the gardens, a quieter spot set back from the river. Each has a queen bed in the open-plan living area and a second bedroom for the kids.

The kitchen is fully equipped and there's a washing machine. Cottage 4 also welcomes small dogs, so the whole family can come.
