Small dogs are welcome here.

Two of our ten cottages are kept for guests travelling with a small dog. There's lawn to stretch out on, the river to sniff along and the Rail Trail across the road for a proper walk.

We ask a few things of dog owners, mostly so the wildlife and the other guests stay as relaxed as you are.

The dog rules

Draft wording for Steve and Narelle to confirm

  • On a lead outside

    Please keep your dog on a lead anywhere in the grounds, including the lawns and the river bank.

  • Never left alone

    Dogs can't be left in the cottage by themselves. If you're heading out, they come too.

  • Off the beds

    Bring your dog's own bed or blanket, and keep them off the beds and sofas.

  • Clean up

    Pick up after your dog everywhere on the property and use the bins.

  • Keep it quiet

    Other guests come here for the peace. A dog that barks a lot may not enjoy it, and nor will they.

  • Why the lead matters

    Wallabies, echidnas, possums and parrots live in these gardens. A lead keeps them safe, and keeps your dog out of trouble too.

Out and about

Where to take them

  • The Rail TrailAcross the road. Flat, sealed and good for a long walk on a lead.
  • The river bank200 metres of it, right here, on a lead.
  • Bright8 km away, with riverside paths through town.
  • Not Mount BuffaloDogs aren't allowed in the national park, so plan that day without them.
