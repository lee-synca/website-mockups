What groups use

The things that make it easier when there are a lot of you.

A covered place to gather

Free barbecues, tables and a pool table under one roof, whatever the weather.

The Rail Trail across the road

Ride or walk to Bright without loading a single bike onto a car.

Lawns and the river

Space to spread out, with 200 metres of river bank to yourselves.

Bike storage

Somewhere on site to keep the bikes overnight.

A ski drying room

For winter groups back from Mount Buffalo.

A laundry

Shared facilities, useful on a longer stay.

Who comes

Walking groups

Stay several nights and walk a different track each day, then cook together in the evening. Mount Buffalo and the river paths are close.

Cycling clubs

Roll out of the gate onto the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail, or use us as a base for the mountain climbs.

Families and friends

A reunion, a big birthday or a long weekend. Grandparents in a studio, the cousins in the bunks.

How to plan it

Groups are easier by phone or message than through the booking page.

  1. Tell us your dates and numbers

    Roughly how many people, how many couples and families, and when you'd like to come.

  2. We work out the cottages

    We'll suggest who goes where and hold the cottages for you while you check with the group.
