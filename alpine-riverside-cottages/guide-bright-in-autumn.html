Autumn is when the valley is at its most beautiful. The avenues of exotic trees planted through Bright turn gold, orange and red, and so do the trees along our own river bank.

When to come

The leaves start to turn in late March. The colour usually peaks around the last week of April and the first week of May, though the weather moves it a little each year: a hot, dry season or a windy week can bring it on early or strip the trees fast. Book early for those weeks, as they're among our busiest.

Days are often mild and still, mornings are crisp, and the light is soft all day. It's lovely walking and riding weather.

The Bright Autumn Festival

The festival is run by volunteers through the Bright & District Chamber of Commerce and timed for the peak of the colour. In 2027 it runs from Friday 23 April to Sunday 2 May.

  • Gala Day is Saturday 1 May 2027: the Al Findlay Memorial parade, a large market and music in the streets.
  • Open gardens in and around Bright, through the festival.
  • An autumn art exhibition, historical tours, talks and sport.

The Wandiligong Nut Festival, up the valley from Bright, is held around the same time. Check the festival's own website for the full program closer to the date.

Getting there from here

Bright is 8 km from our gate. On a festival weekend, leave the car at the cottage and ride or walk the Rail Trail into town. You'll see the colour all along the way, and you won't need to hunt for a park on Gala Day.

Back at the cottages, light the barbecue and eat outside among the fallen leaves. The barbecues are free to use.

Before you go

Good to know

  • Book ahead. The peak weeks and the festival fill up early, here and in town.
  • Mornings are cold. Bring a warm layer. Afternoons are often mild and still.
  • Colour varies. A hot or windy season can bring it on early. Ring us and we'll tell you how the trees look.
  • Festival dates. Check the program before you travel. It's run by volunteers and details can change.
  • Dogs. Welcome on a lead on the Rail Trail. Two of our cottages take small dogs.
