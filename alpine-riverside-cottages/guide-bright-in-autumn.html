Autumn is when the valley is at its most beautiful. The trees in Bright turn gold, orange and red, and so do the ones along our own river bank.

When to come

The leaves start to turn in late March. The colour usually peaks around the last week of April and the first week of May, though the weather moves it a little each year. Book early for those weeks, as they're among our busiest.

The Bright Autumn Festival

The festival is run by volunteers from the Bright & District Chamber of Commerce and timed for the peak of the colour. In 2027 it runs from Friday 23 April to Sunday 2 May.

The big day is the Gala Day on Saturday 1 May, with a parade, a large market and music in the streets. Through the festival there are open gardens, an art exhibition, historical tours and talks. Check the festival's own website for the full program closer to the time.

Getting there from here

Bright is 8 km from our gate. On a festival weekend, leave the car at the cottage and ride or walk the Rail Trail into town. You'll get the colour along the way, too.

Before you go

Good to know

  • Book ahead. The peak weeks and the festival fill up early, here and in town.
  • Mornings are cold. Bring a warm layer. Afternoons are often mild and still.
  • Colour varies. A hot or windy season can bring it on early. Ring us and we'll tell you how the trees look.
  • Festival dates. The 2027 dates are from the festival's own website. Check the program there before you travel.
  • Dogs. Welcome on a lead on the Rail Trail. Two of our cottages take small dogs.
