povi·boy

/ˈpo.vi.bɔɪ/  noun

  1. From povi, the Samoan word for cow. A cowboy, said our way.
  2. A townie who swapped Nikes for gumboots and learned to rope, ride and write it all down.See also: Samoan Cowboy, Big Shrimpp.
  3. The debut album by TheWesternGuide. Ten tracks, 29 minutes, out 20 November 2026.

Aaron Pulemagafa grew up singing in church in West Auckland, on a diet of Kenny Rogers and George Strait.

He had hits in Samoan first. Pe Moni Ea passed a million streams, and Siva Mai screened at Māoriland Film Festival. Then he picked up country and it fit. Guitar, a voice and a story is how Samoan music has always worked.

Since then he's opened for Kaylee Bell, played CMC Rocks in Queensland, and cut a duet with her for this record. He still calls himself a townie.
