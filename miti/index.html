The story

Songs that mean what they say

There's a particular kind of country song that doesn't try too hard, the kind that just tells the truth and lets the melody do the rest. That's the space Tiana Miti writes from.

Tiana grew up on her mother's cassette tapes of Glen Campbell and Johnny Cash, the kind of songs that get stuck in your bones before you're old enough to understand why. Raised in Porirua, she came up around music the way most people come up around family: always there, never separate from everyday life.

That early foundation became the backbone of MITI – classic country storytelling, plainspoken and honest, with real instruments, straightforward lyrics and a voice that leans into feeling over polish. Her brother Sonny writes and arranges behind the scenes.

Country music has never had a strong foothold in New Zealand, but that's beginning to shift, and MITI wants to be part of the reason why. Not by chasing a trend, but by writing songs that are simple, true and built to last, one release at a time.
