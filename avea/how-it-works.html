Step one – your foundation

Apply & onboard

Getting to know your music

Start by telling us about your music, your plans and what you'd like help with.

  • Apply to join

    Share your music and goals through a short application. There's no fee to apply – just a conversation about where you're headed.

  • We review the fit

    We listen properly and look at where you are now. If AVEA is the right partner for your next chapter, we'll say so – honestly.

  • Onboarding & setup

    We set up your accounts, rights and profiles the right way – clean metadata, correct splits, and your masters kept with you under our standard distribution terms.

02

Step two – your launch

Plan & release

Your music, everywhere it matters

Once we've agreed on the services, we'll get the release information sorted and work through the timing and promotion.

  • Build the release plan

    Timing, rollout, pitching and positioning – a data-informed strategy designed to give each release its best possible start.

  • Deliver the release

    Your music goes to major streaming and download platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok and YouTube – delivered properly.

  • Promote & amplify

    We support the launch with marketing and pitching, and can set up YouTube monetisation and Content ID so eligible uses of your music can earn where the rights allow.

03

Step three – the long game

Grow together

A career, not just a release

After release, we'll look at performance and royalty reporting, and talk about what could work better next time.

  • Track & learn

    Transparent reporting shows what's working and why, so every next move is sharper than the last.

  • Collect what you're owed

    We help with royalty reporting, and can discuss additional rights services – publishing, neighbouring rights and sync – where they apply.

  • Build the career

    Audience growth, brand, networking and long-term development that compounds with every release.

What stays true at every stage

The way we work never changes.

  • You keep your masters

    You keep ownership of your music under our standard distribution terms. We'll be clear about the permissions we need.

  • Transparent, always

    We want you to understand your reports and what you've earned.

  • One partner, one team

    We can help with distribution, YouTube and release support, depending on the services you choose.

Common questions

Before you apply.

A few common questions before you get started.

Does AVEA take ownership of my music?

Under our standard distribution terms, you keep your masters. We'll explain any licences and permissions in the agreement.

How much does it cost to apply?

Nothing. There's no fee to apply and no obligation to sign up. Our standard services use a revenue-share model, and we'll explain the relevant fees and splits before you agree to anything.

Do I need a finished release to join?

No. We work with artists at different stages and can talk about what support would be useful for you.

Where will my music be available?

We help deliver your releases to major streaming and download platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok and YouTube, and we'll work with you to get the artwork, credits and release details right.
