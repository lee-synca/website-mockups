Our services

Three ways we help your channel.

There's more to YouTube than uploading videos. We help with the details that make your content easier to find, bring new viewers to your channel and make sure eligible uses of your work are earning for you.

01

Video & Asset Optimisation

We look at what's already on your channel and find ways to give it a better chance of being discovered. That includes new uploads and older videos that still have an audience to reach.

  • Improve video titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • Organise playlists around what your audience likes to watch.
  • Refresh older videos and catalogue content so they stay relevant.
02

Network Growth & Cross-Promotion

Your channel doesn't have to grow on its own. We look for ways to introduce your content to audiences across the wider network, where the fit makes sense.

  • Connect videos through playlists, end screens and cards.
  • Explore collaborations and cross-promotion with relevant channels.
  • Support releases, events and campaigns that bring viewers back to your channel.
03

UGC & Third-Party Revenue Collection

When other people use your content on YouTube, there may be revenue you're not collecting. We use Content ID to identify eligible uses, manage claims and help you get paid where the rights allow.

  • Monitor eligible re-uploads, fan videos, reactions and compilations.
  • Claim and collect eligible third-party revenue through Content ID.
  • Handle unauthorised uses thoughtfully, while allowing fan activity that helps your reach where appropriate.
