Overview

AVEA is a Pacific-first independent music company. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website or apply to join AVEA, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have. We keep this in plain language on purpose.

Not legal advice. This policy is provided for transparency. It should be reviewed by a qualified legal advisor before you rely on it, and tailored to AVEA’s final operating details.

Who we are

In this policy, “AVEA”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to AVEA, a Pacific-first music distribution and artist-growth company operating across Aotearoa New Zealand, Australia and the wider Pacific.

For the purposes of the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, AVEA is the agency responsible for the personal information described here. Where the Australian Privacy Act 1988 or other regional privacy laws apply to you, we aim to meet those standards as well.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to review applications, provide our services and run our business. This includes:

  • Application details – your artist name, your name, email, phone, region, genre and the goals you share with us.
  • Music & catalogue information – links to your music and profiles, your release history and details about your current distribution or rights setup.
  • Communications – messages you send us and our correspondence with you.
  • Technical data – basic information your browser provides, such as device and usage data, collected through cookies and analytics (see below).

You don’t have to give us everything – but if you leave out required application details, we may not be able to review your application properly.

How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • review your application and decide whether AVEA is the right fit;
  • contact you about your application and, if you join, provide our services;
  • set up and manage distribution, monetisation, rights and reporting on your behalf;
  • respond to your enquiries and provide support;
  • improve our website, services and communications; and
  • meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don’t use it to make guarantees about playlists, streams or income – that’s not how we work.

Why we’re allowed to use it

We collect and use your information because you’ve chosen to apply or work with us, because it’s necessary to provide services you’ve asked for, to meet our legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in running an independent music company responsibly. Where the law requires your consent – for example, certain marketing – we’ll ask for it, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it’s needed to deliver our services, and always under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection arrangements. This may include:

  • Digital platforms & stores – such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and TikTok, where we deliver and manage your music at your request;
  • Service providers – trusted partners who help us with hosting, analytics, royalty collection, accounting and communications;
  • Professional advisors – such as legal and financial advisors, where necessary; and
  • Authorities – where we’re required to by law.

We don’t hand your information to advertisers or data brokers.

Where your information is stored

Your information may be stored and processed in New Zealand, Australia or in other countries where our service providers operate. Some of the platforms we work with are based overseas. Where information is transferred outside your country, we take reasonable steps to ensure it’s protected by comparable safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as required by law – for example, tax and financial records. If your application isn’t successful, we keep your details for a reasonable period in case a future opportunity fits, then securely delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see Your rights).

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct anything that’s wrong or out of date;
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we’re not required to keep it;
  • Withdraw consent to marketing at any time; and
  • Make a complaint if you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. In New Zealand you may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner; in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Cookies & analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to help it work properly and to understand how visitors use it, so we can improve it. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings – though some parts of the site may not work as well if you do.

Children

Our services are aimed at artists building a career and are not directed at children. If you’re under 18, please involve a parent or guardian before applying or sharing personal information with us. If you believe a child has provided us information without appropriate consent, contact us and we’ll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “last updated” date at the top of this page, and where changes are significant, we’ll take reasonable steps to let you know.
