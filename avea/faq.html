01 – Getting Started

Joining AVEA

Who we're for, how to apply, and what happens once you do.

Who can apply?

AVEA is built for Pacific artists – across Aotearoa, Australia, Fiji, Tonga, Samoa and the wider Pacific and its diaspora. If you're making music and you're serious about your career, we want to hear from you at any stage.

Do I need a finished release to join?

Not at all. Whether you're preparing your very first single or already releasing regularly, we'll meet you where you are and build the plan around your goals – not the other way around.

How do I apply?

Start with the application from any page on this site. Tell us about your music, your story and where you want to go. There's no fee to apply – it's simply the start of a conversation.

How long does the process take?

We'll review your application and get back to you. Timing depends on the number of applications and what you need help with. If it's a fit, we'll talk through onboarding and the timing of your first release together.

What happens after I'm accepted?

We onboard you properly – setting up your accounts, rights and profiles with clean metadata and correct splits – then build your first release plan together. You can see the full journey on our How It Works page.

02 – Money & Rights

Ownership & cost

The part that matters most – told straight, with nothing hidden.

Do I keep my rights and my masters?

Under our standard distribution arrangement, you keep ownership of your masters. We'll explain the rights we need to work with your music before you sign.

What does it cost to join AVEA?

There's no fee to apply. Our standard services use a revenue-share model, and we'll explain the relevant fees and splits before you agree to anything.

How and when do I get paid?

You receive transparent reporting on what you've earned and why. How and when royalties are paid will be set out in your agreement before you sign.

Which royalties does AVEA collect for me?

We help with royalty reporting and rights information, and can discuss additional rights services – publishing, neighbouring rights and sync – where they apply.

Is there a lock-in contract?

Our agreements are clear and fair, and we'll walk you through every term before anything is signed. Nothing is hidden, and you'll always understand exactly what you're agreeing to.

03 – Releases & Distribution

Getting your music out

Where your music lands, how it gets there, and moving from your current setup.

Where will my music be available?

We help deliver your releases to major streaming and download platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok and YouTube, and we'll work with you to get the artwork, credits and release details right.

Do I have to leave my current distributor?

Not necessarily. We can talk through your current agreement and what a move might involve before any changes are made.

Can you help with YouTube monetisation?

Yes. We can help set up channel monetisation, and use Content ID to identify eligible uses of your music across YouTube, manage claims and help you get paid where the rights allow.

Do you help plan the release itself?

Absolutely. We build a data-informed plan around timing, rollout, pitching and positioning – designed to give each release its best possible start and a long life afterwards.

04 – Growth & Support

Beyond the release

How we keep building your audience, income and career over time.

What kind of support do I get after a release?

The work doesn't stop when a track goes live. We track performance, help with royalty reporting and keep building your audience and opportunities – release after release.

Do you help grow my audience and brand?

Yes. Marketing, networking and career development are part of what we do – turning listeners into a lasting fanbase and opening doors that compound over time.

Who will I actually be working with?

One team – distribution, rights, strategy and growth in the same place, so you always know who to talk to.

My question isn't answered here – what now?

Reach out any time. Start an application or contact us directly and we'll answer honestly – even if the honest answer is that we're not the right fit for you yet.
