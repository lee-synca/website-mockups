Your music, everywhere it matters

Distribution

We help deliver your releases to major streaming and download platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube Music. We'll work with you to get the artwork, credits and release details right.

  • Global reachMajor streaming and download platforms, one delivery.
  • Clean metadataCredits, artwork and release info done right.
  • Release schedulingCoordinated drops across your chosen platforms.

Grow your channel. Make more of your content.

YouTube Services

Your YouTube channel can do more than you think. We help you improve how your videos are found, reach more people through our network and collect eligible revenue from uses of your content across YouTube.

  • Video optimisationImprove titles, thumbnails, metadata and playlists.
  • Audience growthConnect with viewers through playlists, collaborations and cross-promotion.
  • Content IDIdentify eligible third-party uses and collect revenue where available.

Get paid what you're owed

Rights & Royalties

You should know what you own and where your income comes from. We help with royalty reporting and rights information, and can discuss additional rights services where they apply. Your ownership stays subject to the agreement you choose.

  • Your mastersUnder our standard distribution arrangement, you keep ownership of your masters.
  • Additional rights servicesPublishing, neighbouring rights and sync, discussed where they apply.
  • Transparent reportingClarity on what you've earned and why.
