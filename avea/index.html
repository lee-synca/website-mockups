The deal

The deal, in plain words.

Before you send us anything, here is how working with AVEA goes.

  • There's no fee to apply.

    Our standard services use a revenue-share model, and we'll explain the relevant fees and splits before you agree to anything.

  • Under our standard distribution arrangement, you keep ownership of your masters.

    We'll explain the rights we need to work with your music before you sign.

  • Nothing is signed until you understand it.

    We'll talk you through the terms and splits first. Bring your family, your manager or your lawyer.

  • We won't promise streams, playlists or fame.

    Nobody honestly can. What we offer is proper work, straight answers and clear reporting.

Questions

Before you ask – the honest answers.

Here are a few things you might want to know before getting in touch.

Still curious? Talk to us
Do I keep my rights and my masters?

Under our standard distribution arrangement, you keep ownership of your masters. We'll explain the rights we need to work with your music before you sign.

What does it cost to join AVEA?

There's no fee to apply. Our standard services use a revenue-share model, and we'll explain the relevant fees and splits before you agree to anything.

Who can apply?

We work with artists from across the Pacific and its communities around the world. Whether you're just getting started or have an established catalogue, we'd like to hear what you're doing.

How long does the process take?

We'll review your application and get back to you. Timing depends on the number of applications and what you need help with.

Do I have to leave my current distributor?

Not necessarily. We can talk through your current agreement and what a move might involve before any changes are made.
